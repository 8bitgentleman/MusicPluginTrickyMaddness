Director's console — stands in for the game/Python side

Idle capsule is 56px — matches the concept's scale. Once open it stays open: the transport controls never collapse it, only a click outside the panel, Esc, or 5s of the pointer being away from it. There are no pop-up notifications: a track changing while the capsule is idle just flicks a single cyan ring off its border, and a change while it's open rides the title's slide. The queue below cycles four mock tracks including one tagged CUSTOM, standing in for a track picked up from a user's own radio/custom/ folder once the Python side globs it like the other three soundtracks.
